About Me

I'm a Backend Developer passionate about building reliable, scalable, and maintainable web applications.

I recently completed my Informatics Engineering degree and gained practical experience through internships as a Junior Web Developer and QC Intern. These opportunities allowed me to work on backend development, API implementation, database management, software testing, debugging, and technical documentation while collaborating within professional development teams.

I enjoy designing backend systems that not only function well but are also maintainable and aligned with real business needs. I believe that great software is built through continuous learning, collaboration, and a strong understanding of both technology and business requirements. My goal is to grow as a Backend Developer and contribute to building software that creates meaningful impact.
